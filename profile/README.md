# Privatus Analytics

**Real visitor numbers for your website. No cookies, no cookie pop-up, no bots.**

You want to know how many people visit your site and where they come from. But
Google Analytics uses cookies, so in many countries you must show a cookie
pop-up first (the box that asks visitors to say yes to cookies). Many people
click no, so they never show up in your numbers. Bots add fake visits on top.

Privatus Analytics counts visits without cookies and keeps no IP addresses.
Bots are filtered out before they are counted.

So you see how many real people came, which pages they looked at and where they
came from, on one simple dashboard. A report comes to your inbox each week, and
you can set alerts for when traffic jumps or drops. And you don't need a cookie
pop-up for your analytics. Laws differ by country, so check your own legal
advice.

**Free plan, no card:** 10,000 events a month (an event is a page view or an
action you track) on up to 3 sites.
[Start free at privatusanalytics.com](https://privatusanalytics.com).
